Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Gauge, published by Waikuu, handles your information. Gauge works entirely on your device: there is no account to create and no server that stores your data.

1. Data stored on your device

Your hydration logs, your goal and your settings are stored only on your device. This data is never transmitted to us or to any server, and we never have access to it. Deleting the app removes this data.

2. Apple Health

If you enable Health syncing, Gauge writes your logged water intake to Apple Health. Gauge only writes to Apple Health and never reads your Health data. This information stays on your device under Apple's protection and is never sent to us. You can revoke this access at any time in the iOS Health app or Settings.

3. Advertising

Gauge shows ads through Google AdMob. Depending on your choices, these ads may be personalized using your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). Before any personalized advertising, Gauge asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and, in the EU, the UK and Switzerland, a Google consent message. If you decline, you still see ads, but non-personalized ones. Your data is never sold. Google's handling of advertising data is governed by its own privacy policy.

Removing ads

A one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase permanently removes ads. Purchases are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment information.

4. Analytics

Gauge may rely on anonymized usage and crash data (e.g. Apple App Analytics, crash reporting) to improve the app. This data cannot be used to personally identify you and follows the providers' own privacy policies.

5. Your rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, port, and object to the processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@waikuu.com.

6. Children

Gauge is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. Any significant change will be posted on this page.

8. Contact

For any question about this privacy policy: support@waikuu.com.
