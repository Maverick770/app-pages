Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

This policy describes how Mesuro, published by Waikuu, handles your information. Mesuro is a BMI calculator and body-composition tracker designed with your privacy in mind.

1. Principle: everything stays on your device

Mesuro runs entirely on your device. The app has no server and never transmits any of your health or profile data to a remote server. Your measurements, history, profile and goals are stored locally (a SwiftData database and device preferences), in a container shared with the Mesuro widget on your device. You retain sole control.

2. Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your explicit permission, and only if you enable it, Mesuro integrates with Apple Health:

  • Read: your weight and height are read from Apple Health to pre-fill your measurements and calculate your BMI and body composition.
  • Write: the weight and height you record in Mesuro are written to Apple Health so you can track your progress over time.

Your health data never leaves your device through us. Data from Apple Health is stored on your device and managed by Apple. Mesuro never transmits it to any server, never uses it for advertising or marketing, and never shares it with any third party, data broker, or analytics service.

You can grant or revoke this access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Mesuro. Revoking access does not delete data already stored in Apple Health, which you manage from the Health app.

3. Data processed locally

To function, Mesuro keeps on your device: your measurement history (BMI, weight, height, category), your profile (age, biological sex, activity level), any body measurements (waist, neck, hips, calf), your weight goals, and your tracking streak. This information is neither collected by the publisher nor shared with third parties.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Mesuro offers a one-time purchase and a Premium subscription managed by Apple through your App Store account. Payment processing is handled exclusively by Apple: we never have access to your payment details. We only receive, from Apple, the anonymous confirmation of your Premium status required to unlock features.

5. Notifications

If you enable them, Mesuro schedules weigh-in reminders locally on your device using iOS notifications. No notification is sent from a server, and no data is collected in the process.

6. No advertising, no trackers

Mesuro contains no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no third-party trackers. No advertising identifier is used.

7. Children

Mesuro is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

8. Your rights (GDPR)

As your data is stored locally, you can view, edit, or delete it directly within the app (including full data deletion from settings). Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, port, and object to the processing of your personal data. For any request: abitbol.laurent@gmail.com.

9. Health disclaimer

Mesuro is an information and tracking tool. It does not provide a medical diagnosis and does not replace professional medical advice. Discuss your results with your doctor.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated. Any significant change will be posted on this page, with the date above updated accordingly.

11. Contact

For any question about this privacy policy: abitbol.laurent@gmail.com.
